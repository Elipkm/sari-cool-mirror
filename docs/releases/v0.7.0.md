# v0.7.0 — Strategy Decision

## Goal

Decide objectively whether the frozen `trend-pullback-v1` strategy has enough historical evidence of an edge to continue unchanged.

## Frozen protocol

The periods, thresholds and verdict rules were committed to `docs/validation/v0.7-criteria.md` before the real evaluation was run.

- Three consecutive, non-overlapping 180-day periods
- Development, validation and out-of-sample labels
- Completed Kraken daily EUR candles for BTC, ETH and SOL
- v0.6.0 strategy, risk rules, 0.25% fees and 0.10% slippage
- Equal-weight BTC/ETH/SOL buy-and-hold benchmark
- Five explicit acceptance criteria
- Predetermined ACCEPT, REVISE and REJECT boundaries

## Delivered

- Bounded historical simulation with explicit start and end dates.
- `POST /api/validation/evaluate` runs the complete frozen protocol.
- Automatic BULL, BEAR and SIDEWAYS regime classification from benchmark return.
- Dashboard report showing all three periods, benchmark excess return, drawdown, closed trades, PASS/FAIL criteria and final verdict.
- Validation remains isolated from the persistent paper ledger.

## Real result

| Period | Regime | Strategy | Benchmark | Excess | Max drawdown | Closed trades |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Development | BULL | -1.45% | +50.33% | -51.78% | 2.47% | 10 |
| Validation | BEAR | -1.76% | -49.47% | +47.71% | 2.38% | 4 |
| Out of sample | BULL | +3.34% | +20.38% | -17.04% | 2.89% | 17 |

Acceptance criteria:

- PASS: positive out-of-sample return
- FAIL: out-of-sample return meets or beats benchmark
- PASS: out-of-sample drawdown is at most 10%
- PASS: at least five out-of-sample completed trades
- FAIL: positive return in at least two of three periods

## Verdict: REVISE

The strategy controls drawdown and avoided most of the bear-market loss, but it did not demonstrate a sufficient return edge:

- only one period was profitable
- both bull periods substantially underperformed buy-and-hold
- closed-trade win rates were low
- the strategy's defensive behavior is useful, but the entry/exit rules capture too little upside

The strategy must not progress unchanged toward live trading. Any later revision must address this measured weakness without tuning thresholds against the same out-of-sample period. The original results remain the permanent baseline.

## Proof

`StrategyValidationEndToEndTest` starts the Spring application with deterministic market history, runs all three frozen periods through HTTP, proves each period contains exactly 180 days with no overlap, proves five criteria produce an explicit REJECT verdict, and confirms the paper-trade ledger remains untouched.

The complete backend test suite, Angular production build, real Kraken evaluation through nginx, and visual dashboard check pass before release.
