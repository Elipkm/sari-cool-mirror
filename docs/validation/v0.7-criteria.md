# v0.7 Frozen Strategy-Decision Protocol

This protocol is fixed before running the v0.7 evaluation. Results must not change the periods, thresholds or verdict rules.

## Frozen strategy

- Strategy: `trend-pullback-v1`
- Assets: BTC, ETH and SOL
- Candles: completed Kraken daily EUR candles
- Starting capital per period: €5,000
- Fee: 0.25% per execution
- Slippage: 0.10% per execution
- Risk configuration: the committed v0.6.0 deterministic risk policy
- Benchmark: equal-weight BTC/ETH/SOL buy-and-hold

No strategy or risk parameter may be optimized during this evaluation.

## Non-overlapping periods

Dates are derived from the latest completed UTC day when the evaluation runs:

1. **Development:** 540 through 361 days before today
2. **Validation:** 360 through 181 days before today
3. **Out of sample:** 180 through 1 day before today

Earlier candles may warm up the 20-day and 50-day averages, but trades and results are counted only inside each period.

Each period is classified from its benchmark return:

- **BULL:** greater than +10%
- **BEAR:** less than -10%
- **SIDEWAYS:** between -10% and +10%, inclusive

## Acceptance criteria

All five must pass:

1. Out-of-sample strategy return is positive.
2. Out-of-sample strategy return is at least the benchmark return.
3. Out-of-sample maximum drawdown is at most 10%.
4. Out-of-sample period contains at least five completed trades.
5. Strategy return is positive in at least two of the three periods.

## Verdict

- **ACCEPT:** all five acceptance criteria pass.
- **REJECT:** any period exceeds 15% drawdown, out-of-sample return is -10% or worse, or none of the periods has a positive strategy return.
- **REVISE:** every other result. A revision must respond to a specific measured weakness; thresholds and historical results must remain visible.

This protocol evaluates historical evidence, not guaranteed future profitability.
